Privacy Policy

This page explains, in plain words, how this website handles information. It describes how the site currently works.

What the enquiry form does

When you fill in the bulk enquiry form, your details (name, business name, phone number, optional email, product type, quantity, size and requirements) are used to write a message. That message opens in WhatsApp or your email app so that you can send it to .

  • This website does not store the form details on its own server.
  • Nothing is sent to us until you press Send in WhatsApp or your email app.
  • Once you send a message, it is handled by that app under its own privacy terms.

How we use enquiries you send

Details you send us are used to reply to your enquiry, discuss specifications and prepare a quotation.

Cookies and tracking

The site does not set advertising cookies and does not load third-party analytics or tracking scripts. Fonts are served from this website itself.
